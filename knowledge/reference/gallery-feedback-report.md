### 3.2 问题详解

#### 🔴 致命 1: 缺少编辑功能 (35%)

这是最大的用户痛点。用户期望在图库中有基本的图片编辑能力。

> "no image editing option available."
> "no editing features..there is no pin to draw in pictures..no typing ..no selection and removing items.. nothing in fact related to photos editor.."
> "Its missing most basic feature like drawing on images"
> "It's very strange that this standard gallery that comes preinstalled on Nothing phones doesn't have the simple ability to draw on photos."
> "The gallery app is extremely minimalistic but lacks basic editing features like drawing on pictures to highlight stuff"

**用户要什么**: 涂鸦/画笔、裁剪、文字添加、旋转

#### 🔴 致命 2: AI 擦除被移除 (30%)

用户专门指出 *之前有* 这个功能，更新后消失了。

> "magic eraser not available."
> "There is no option for erase in the image, Before it was available. Kindly add this feature plz."
> "please erase option ko bapas laooi...😕😭😭😭😭"
> "nothing gallery AI future objective remover not available"
> "please at least provide image eraser feature and ai feature like other smartphone company"
> "очень хорошо, приятный интерфейс, удобная сартировка, но куда делось AI удаление объектов на фото ?" *(很喜欢但AI物体删除去哪了？)*

**用户要什么**: 魔术橡皮擦/AI 物体消除 — 这是一个功能回归需求

#### 🔴 严重: 照片丢失/隐藏相册 Bug (18%)

这是数据安全问题，严重度最高。

> " Following a recent gallery application update, all my photos stored in the 'Hidden Folder' have permanently disappeared."
> "nothing os 4.0 I can't find the hidden photos in my Nothing Gallery after the update. Now what do I do to get all my pictures?"
> "i lost all my pictures and videos from the nothing hidden gallery folder I had many important pictures in my hidden gallery and now they're gone"
> "when we want to open private photos from gallery it asks for lock screen password. please make privacy password required instead of lock screen."

**问题**: OS 4.0 升级后隐藏相册数据丢失 + 隐私密码应该独立于锁屏密码

#### 🟡 中等: 相册排序混乱 (12%)

> "There is not sort option available in album tab.. Albums are showing in random order not in a alphabetical order"
> "Photo folders are not listed alphabetically. Please fix this as soon as possible."
> "The albums section just has a list of folders. I can't set a cover picture for an album."

#### 🟡 中等: 加载黑屏/性能 (16%)

> "app often does not actually save photos when accessing the camera, can't transfer between folders without freezing, it's almost unusable"
> "Some photos shows a black screen. I can't even delete them or move to another folder."
> "photos tak time to appear and sometimes simply don't."
